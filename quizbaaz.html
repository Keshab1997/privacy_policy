Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

QuizBaaz ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application QuizBaaz ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

You can use QuizBaaz without creating an account (Guest Mode). If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Name — to personalize your profile and leaderboard
  • Email Address — for account identification
  • Profile Photo — if you choose to use your Google photo as your avatar

1.2 Gameplay Data

When you play quizzes or battles, we store:

  • Quiz results, scores and answer history
  • Coins, gems, XP, level, streaks and other in-game rewards
  • Shop items and inventory purchases (in-game currency only)
  • Leaderboard entries and daily quiz participation

1.3 Avatar Images

If you upload a custom avatar, the image is sent to imgbb.com (a third-party image hosting service) so it can be shown to other players in leaderboards and battles.

1.4 No Real-Money Transactions

QuizBaaz does not process any real-money payments or in-app purchases. All rewards (coins, gems) are earned through gameplay.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the App, including quizzes, battles and leaderboards
  • To display your profile and achievements to you
  • To rank players and publish daily champions
  • To respond to support requests

2.1 Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted by law. You can limit personalized advertising in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising).

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — sign-in, cloud storage of your account and gameplay data
  • Google Sign-In — authentication
  • Google AdMob — serving advertisements (see Section 2.1)
  • imgbb.com — hosting of custom avatar images
  • Google Play — distribution of the App

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Cloud (Firebase). Access to your data is restricted by Firebase security rules so that only you can modify your own account data.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and gameplay data for as long as your account exists. Data is permanently deleted when you delete your account (see Section 6).

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the App: Profile → Delete Account → type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your profile, quiz history, leaderboard entries, coins, gems, rewards and shop inventory.
  • Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.

For help with deletion, contact us through the developer's GitHub profile.

7. Children's Privacy

The App may be used by students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via the developer's GitHub profile.
